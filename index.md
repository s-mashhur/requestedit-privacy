RequestEdit Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

RequestEdit is a Jira Service Management Forge application that allows customers to edit permitted fields on their own service requests.

Data Processing

RequestEdit processes Jira and Jira Service Management data only as required to provide its functionality.

The application may process:
- Jira request information
- Editable request field values
- Atlassian account identifiers required to verify the requester
- Audit information related to edits made through RequestEdit

Data Storage

RequestEdit does not operate an external database or external storage service.

Application data is processed within the Atlassian Forge and Jira environment. Audit information generated by RequestEdit is stored within Jira.

Third-Party Sharing

RequestEdit does not sell or share user data with third-party advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Security

RequestEdit uses Atlassian Forge APIs and Jira permissions to restrict request editing to authorized users.
